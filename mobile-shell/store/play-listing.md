## Product facts

Brainamon is a local library for saved links, notes and canvases. Current
application-owned source is GPL-3.0-only. All current features are available
without a payment or receipt. Optional support: https://ko-fi.com/fredtomas.
Redistribution and modification follow LICENSE and third-party component terms.

Windows downloads and future source releases:
https://github.com/knackychan/brainamon/releases. Do not describe an unverified
historical installer as built from the newly published source.

Optional AI uses the user's own provider credentials or configured endpoint.
Relevant content can leave the device; provider charges and terms apply
separately. Google Drive integration requires the user's Google account.
Standalone phone backup and trusted PC pairing do not require a purchase.
Paired Drive access uses a signed one-hour grant renewed by the running PC;
downloaded content remains readable offline. Older phone apps may need an
update; existing legacy receipt handling remains for compatibility.

Support and privacy requests: me@fredtomas.com. Read the in-app Privacy policy
for processing, storage and deletion details. Optional support payments use the
external support service's checkout. The application has no card-entry form.
Historical marketplace transactions retain their applicable terms; this draft
does not change those terms or promise refunds, service duration or future updates.

## Phone full description

Save links and notes on your phone, import Instagram Saved ZIP/JSON, and organize
available content. Sharing creates a draft; review it and tap Save. Optional
own-key Wiskit AI answers questions using your content and source references.
Provider charges and terms apply separately.

Use the phone on its own or pair with your Windows library. Optional Google Drive
sync stores encrypted content in your Google account; paired devices hold
decryption keys, and Google can see file sizes and update metadata. Read saved
content offline. AI, previews and sync need a network connection. Downloads,
scraping and video analysis run on Windows; direct-PC mode needs the PC running
and reachable. Previews and media availability depend on the source site.

All current features are available without a payment or receipt. Optional
support helps the developer continue work. Read the source license and Privacy
policy in More. Unpairing or clearing a cache does not erase all device/cloud
copies; uninstalling Windows preserves desktop data by default.

## Publication gates

- Official package: app.brainamon.mobile; local debug builds use the distinct
  app.brainamon.mobile.contributor identity and SDK-local signing key.
- Android GPL binary distribution remains held for exact Google SDK license
  compatibility, corresponding source/notices and payload review. A successful
  contributor compilation is not distribution clearance.
- Production signing, physical-device qualification, developer-account
  verification, current Console policy declarations and a stable public policy
  URL need evidence before a Play release. No Console changes were made here.
- Complete the actual content-rating and Data safety questionnaires against the
  delivered APK and current Google definitions. Do not infer "none collected"
  from the absence of developer-hosted library storage.
- Verify any external support link against current store rules before inclusion.
  No current-rule compliance claim is made by this source draft.
- Use owned sample content for screenshots. The editable icon generator is
  scripts/build_brand.py; source inputs are documented in assets-source/README.md.
